# Word Sprint

Timed writing sprints on the open note.

**Commands:** `Start word sprint`, `Stop word sprint`, `Set sprint word target` (100/300/500/750/1000), `Set sprint length` (5/10/15/25/30 min).

**How it works:** starting a sprint snapshots the open note's word count; the status bar shows `123/300 · 12:34 left` as you write. Hit the target and you're congratulated; run out of time and you get your final tally. Switching to a different note mid-sprint shows "paused" instead of counting the wrong note.

Pack: `node plugins/pack.mjs plugins/oem/word-sprint`
